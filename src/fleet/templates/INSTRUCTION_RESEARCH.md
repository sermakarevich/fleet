# Fleet Task Protocol — researching a job

Read the goal (the epic title and description). Explore the repository
under the invocation directory: structure, tests, conventions, related
code. Write `$FLEET_TASK_DIR/artifacts/RESEARCH.md` (max 12 KB): what exists, what must
change, risks, files involved. Do not change code. Write `$FLEET_TASK_DIR/RESULT.json`
with `status="partial"` and `next_step="design"`.
